# Product Safety & Compliance

## Overview

Amazon requires sellers to ensure that all products comply with applicable safety standards and regulations. Different product categories and different countries/regions have different certification requirements. If compliance documents cannot be provided, the listing may be removed.

---

## United States Requirements (Amazon.com)

### FCC Certification (Federal Communications Commission)
**Scope**: All electronic/electrical products, especially those with wireless functionality (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, etc.)

| Type | Applicable Products | Requirement |
|------|---------|------|
| **FCC-SDOC** | Electronic products without wireless functionality | Testing + declaration of conformity |
| **FCC-ID** | Products with wireless functionality (Bluetooth, Wi-Fi) | Register an FCC ID through a TCB agency |

- **Timeline**: 2-4 weeks (SDoC), 2-3 weeks (ID)
- **Cost**: approximately $1,000-$3,000
- **US agent**: required
- **2025 note**: All wireless devices must have a registered FCC ID, otherwise the listing will be removed

### FDA Certification (Food and Drug Administration)
**Scope**: Food, dietary supplements, medical devices, cosmetics, laser products

| Category | Key Requirements | Timeline | Cost Range |
|------|---------|------|---------|
| Medical devices (510k) | Class II premarket notification | 6-12 months | $35K-$80K |
| Food/dietary supplements | Facility registration + prior notice (PN) | 2-4 weeks | $1.5K-$3K |
| Cosmetics (MoCRA 2023) | Facility registration + product listing + safety report | 3-6 months | $5K+ per product |

**2025 Update Highlights**:
- Full enforcement of cosmetics MoCRA compliance
- FSMA HACCP/PC plans mandatory for high-risk food products
- Medical device 510(k) fee is approximately $22,402 (2025 rate)

### CPC Certification (Children's Product Certificate)
**Scope**: All products intended for children aged 12 and under (toys, baby products, children's clothing, learning materials)

- **Standards**: ASTM F963 (toy safety) + CPSIA requirements
- **Must include**: Third-party lab test reports from a CPSC-accepted laboratory
- **Key 2025 updates**:
  - **Annual retesting** (effective September 3, 2025): All children's toys must undergo document verification by an accepted TIC body every year
  - **CPSIA §108 phthalate testing** (6 phthalates, limit 0.1%) is a mandatory item in the report
  - **Tracking label**: A permanent label containing the manufacturer's name, production date, and batch number
- **Timeline**: 1-2 weeks (testing)
- **Cost**: approximately $500-$2,000

### UL/ETL Certification (Safety)
**Scope**: Plug-in electrical appliances (power adapters, home appliances, lighting)

- **UL**: More widely recognized; requires annual factory inspections (4 per year)
- **ETL**: Same standards as UL, 30-40% lower cost, also requires annual audits
- **Timeline**: 4-8 weeks
- **Cost**: $2K-$20K+
- **2025 note**: Although not legally mandatory, Amazon increasingly requires UL/ETL reports for high-voltage/plug-in products

## European Union Requirements (Amazon EU)

### CE Certification (Mandatory)
**Scope**: Electronics, toys, machinery, PPE, medical devices — 30+ countries

| Directive | Scope |
|------|---------|
| **LVD** (Low Voltage) | AC 50-1000V / DC 75-1500V electrical equipment |
| **EMC** (Electromagnetic Compatibility) | All electronic equipment |
| **RoHS** | Restriction of hazardous substances (10 substances) |
| **ErP/Ecodesign** | Energy-related products (energy label required) |
| **PPE Regulation** | Personal protective equipment |
| **MDR/IVDR** | Medical devices |

**Key 2025 requirements**:
- **EU Responsible Person (RP)**: Mandatory for all CE-marked goods (EU 2019/1020)
- **Technical file + DoC (Declaration of Conformity)** must be available for inspection at any time
- **WEEE registration**: Electronics registered per country
- **GPSR (General Product Safety Regulation)**: Fully enforced as of 2024/2025

### Other EU Certifications

| Certification | Scope |
|------|---------|
| **REACH** | Chemicals and articles containing chemical substances |
| **RoHS** | Electronic/electrical products (restricting lead, mercury, cadmium, etc.) |
| **WEEE** | Electronic waste recycling registration (by country) |
| **GS Mark** | German voluntary safety mark (highly trusted) |

## United Kingdom Requirements (Post-Brexit)

- **UKCA** — Replaces CE for Great Britain (transition period for some goods extends into 2025)
- **UK RoHS** — Equivalent to EU RoHS
- **UK Responsible Person** — A UK responsible person must be designated

## Quick Reference: Certifications by Product Category

| Product Category | US Requirements | EU Requirements |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Electronics** | FCC, UL/ETL | CE (LVD+EMC), RoHS, WEEE |
| **Children's products** | CPC, ASTM F963, CPSIA tracking label | CE (Toy Safety Directive), EN71 |
| **Food/supplements** | FDA registration, facility registration | CE (food contact), REACH |
| **Cosmetics** | FDA MoCRA (facility + product listing) | CPNP notification, CPSR |
| **Medical devices** | FDA 510(k)/PMA, QSR 820 | CE (MDR), EU RP |
| **Wireless/Bluetooth** | FCC-ID | CE (RED Directive) |

## Compliance Best Practices

1. **Use Amazon's compliance reference tool**: Enter product keywords + target country to get the list of required certifications
2. **Choose an ISO 17025-accredited laboratory**: Reports from non-accredited labs may be rejected
3. **Batch certification**: "Family" certification across multiple models can reduce the cost per product
4. **Watch for annual updates**: Especially CPC (2025 new rules) and FDA food facilities (updated every two years)
5. **Monitor Seller Central**: Watch for "compliance request" notifications — failing to respond = listing removal
6. **Keep technical files**: Retain all certification and test documents, as Amazon may request them at any time
