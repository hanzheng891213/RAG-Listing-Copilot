# Shopify Prohibited and Restricted Products

## Overview

Shopify's Acceptable Use Policy (AUP) is binding as part of the Terms of Service and defines the categories that may not be sold as well as restricted products. Violating the AUP (even "unknowingly") can bring product removal, frozen payments, store suspension, or account termination. Note that even if a product does not violate the AUP, Shopify Payments or a third-party payment provider may still refuse it under their own prohibited lists.

---

## Prohibited Products & Services Under the AUP

| Category | Description |
|------|------|
| **Counterfeit goods** | Selling fakes, or unauthorized use or infringement of another party's intellectual property |
| **Illegal products** | Any product or service illegal where the business operates |
| **Controlled/illegal drugs** | Controlled substances, illegal drugs, and related products sold without credentials |
| **Unverified supplements** | Supplements or health products not compliant with applicable regulations |
| **False health/medical claims** | Health or medical products with false claims; prescription drugs without a valid prescription process |
| **Recalled products** | Products subject to a government recall |
| **Weapons and firearms** | Weapons, firearms, and related accessories (when legal compliance is not met) |
| **Self-harm related** | Products and content promoting or encouraging deliberate self-harm |
| **Hate content** | Content promoting hatred, violence, or discrimination |
| **Tools for illegal activity** | Products or services facilitating illegal activity |
| **Restricted goods without age verification** | Adult/age-restricted products without compliant age verification |

> This list is not exhaustive and varies by country/region. Check the AUP and local regulations before adding a new product category.

---

## Restricted Products

Restricted products are not absolutely prohibited but require additional compliance conditions.

| Category | Compliance requirement |
|------|---------|
| **Supplements / health products** | Must comply with target market regulations (such as EU, ANVISA); only authorized health claims; disclaimers required |
| **Medical devices** | Require the corresponding certifications and credentials |
| **E-cigarettes / tobacco** | Restricted or requiring a license in most regions; prohibited in some markets |
| **Alcohol** | Must meet local licensing and age verification requirements |
| **Adult products** | Require age verification and content compliance |
| **Prescription drugs / medicines** | Require a valid prescription process and the corresponding licenses |
| **Veterinary drugs / agricultural inputs** | Must meet industry regulatory requirements |

### Red lines for supplements and health claims
- ❌ Prohibited: disease-related "cure/treat/prevent" statements (such as "treats eczema", "cures arthritis")
- ❌ Prohibited: unproven benefit promises and absolutes ("100% effective", "permanent cure")
- ✅ Allowed: structure/function claims (such as "supports healthy skin", "may help joint comfort") with a compliant disclaimer
- ✅ Recommended: a "claims library" per SKU recording allowed wording, prohibited wording, and required qualifiers

## Intellectual Property & Counterfeit

| Rule | Description |
|------|------|
| **Counterfeiting prohibited** | Do not sell counterfeits or products infringing another party's trademark, copyright, or patent |
| **Authorization requirement** | Using a brand logo, character likeness, or artwork requires a lawful license |
| **Image copyright** | Do not misappropriate another party's product photos, model photos, or brand assets |
| **High-risk practices** | Suggestive descriptions such as "same style" or "dupe" carry significantly higher risk when brand elements are present |
| **Monitoring** | Major brands actively monitor Shopify stores; a single DMCA complaint can trigger account review |
| **Consequences** | Product removal, store suspension or termination, and potential legal liability |

---

## Payment Processor Restrictions

| Point | Description |
|------|------|
| **Separate Shopify Payments list** | Shopify Payments has its own prohibited businesses and products list (including counterfeits and pseudo-pharmaceuticals), varying by country/region |
| **Third-party providers** | Providers such as Stripe often apply standards stricter than local law, especially for suggestive health claims |
| **Practical impact** | A product may be legal yet unacceptable to the payment provider, blocking payment collection; switching gateways is possible, but every provider has its own exclusions |
| **Pseudo-pharmaceutical risk** | Supplements and cosmetics with suggestive health claims are often classified as "pseudo-pharmaceuticals" and restricted |

## Pre-Listing Compliance Checklist

- [ ] Does the product fall into a category explicitly prohibited by the AUP?
- [ ] Does it involve brand elements, character likenesses, or another party's images (requiring authorization)?
- [ ] Does it fall into a restricted category (supplements, medical, alcohol, tobacco, adult, and so on)?
- [ ] Have the target market's laws, regulations, and licensing requirements been checked?
- [ ] Does it involve health claims? Are only authorized claims used? Are disclaimers included?
- [ ] Has the product ever been subject to a government recall?
- [ ] Has the product been confirmed as accepted by Shopify Payments?
- [ ] Are the description, images, reviews, FAQ, and advertising copy all compliant (not just the product page)?

## Consequences of Violation and Enforcement Process

| Enforcement tier | Specific measures |
|---------|---------|
| **Tier 1 (default)** | Notify the merchant and allow self-remediation, typically **at the product level first** |
| **Tier 2** | Require removal of violating content, remove the violating product, block payment collection or fund transfers |
| **Tier 3** | Temporarily restrict admin access, freeze the store |
| **Most severe** | Account termination (violating the Terms of Service can bring **immediate termination**) |

- Shopify states its enforcement approach is: notify first, allow self-remediation, enforce at the product level by default, and treat account-level action as a last resort.
- For counterfeits and serious violations, the buffer tiers may be skipped entirely.
- After an account is restricted or terminated, Shopify notifies the merchant by email or in the admin and provides an **appeal** path, reviewed by a human team.
- A high chargeback rate and abnormal payment fraud indicators are also common reasons for store suspension, just as important as product compliance.

## Listing Compliance Checklist

### Prohibited categories
- [ ] Not in an AUP-prohibited category (counterfeit, illegal, controlled drugs, weapons, self-harm, hate, and so on)
- [ ] Not subject to a government recall
- [ ] No laws where the business operates are violated

### Intellectual property
- [ ] No unauthorized brand logos / characters / assets
- [ ] Product photos and copy are original or licensed
- [ ] No suggestive "same style/dupe" descriptions

### Restricted products
- [ ] Target market licensing and certification requirements checked
- [ ] Age-restricted products have a verification process configured

### Health and medical claims
- [ ] No disease cure/treat/prevent statements
- [ ] Health claims comply with local authorized lists and wording
- [ ] Necessary disclaimers included (such as an FDA statement)

### Payment and account
- [ ] The product is confirmed as accepted by Shopify Payments / the payment provider
- [ ] Description, images, reviews, FAQ, and advertising copy are compliant overall
- [ ] Policy check records retained for use as appeal evidence
