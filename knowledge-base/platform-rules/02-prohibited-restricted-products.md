# Prohibited & Restricted Products

## Overview

Amazon divides products into three tiers: fully prohibited (no approval path), restricted/approval-required (gated categories), and freely sellable. The lists vary slightly by marketplace, so when selling cross-border you must verify the specific policies of the target marketplace one by one.

---

## Fully Prohibited Products (Cannot Be Sold Under Any Circumstances)

The following product categories have **no approval path** on Amazon, and being found selling them will result in immediate account deactivation:

### Illegal and Unsafe Items
- Prescription drugs (except for licensed pharmacies)
- Controlled substances and narcotics
- **Counterfeit goods** (zero tolerance)
- Stolen goods and illegal merchandise
- Recalled products (mandatory recalls by the CPSC or other regulatory agencies)

### Weapons and Explosives
- Firearms and accessories
- Ammunition and explosives
- Automatic knives, switchblades
- Stun guns / tasers (varies by marketplace)

### Adult Content
- Pornographic materials
- Adult videos and magazines
- Sex toys and adult products

### Tobacco and Alcohol
- Cigarettes, cigars, pipe tobacco
- Alcoholic beverages (only licensed distributors may sell them; most sellers cannot pass the review)

### Hazardous Materials
- Flammable liquids
- Compressed gases
- Radioactive materials
- Corrosive substances

## Restricted Product Categories (Gated Categories — Approval Required)

The following categories may be sold, but only after passing Amazon's review and approval:

| Category | Key Requirements |
|------|---------|
| **Collectibles & Fine Art** | Coins, artwork, jewelry, watches, sports memorabilia, stamps |
| **Media & Electronics** | Music/DVD, streaming players, Blu-ray |
| **Baby Products** | Feeding supplies, activity gear, strollers/carriers — CPC safety certification required |
| **Health & Personal Care** | Dietary supplements, medical supplies, CPAP devices |
| **Automotive & Powersports** | Compliant images; refurbished parts require warranty |
| **Beauty Topical Products** | High counterfeit-risk category — brand authorization required |
| **Jewelry** | Fine jewelry requires 1+ year of selling experience, $50K annual revenue, a physical store |
| **Food & Beverage** | Invoices from approved suppliers, FDA compliance |
| **Over-the-Counter Drugs** | FDA registration, GMP compliance, COA |
| **Pet Food & Care** | Approval may be required |

## Applying for Ungating

### Basic Seller Requirements
- **Professional seller account** ($39.99/month) — individual seller accounts cannot sell restricted products
- Good performance metrics:
  - Order Defect Rate (ODR) < 1%
  - Pre-fulfillment cancel rate < 2.5%
  - Late shipment rate < 4%
- Company website (to demonstrate legitimacy)

### Commonly Required Documents
- **Invoices**: Issued within the last 90-180 days, printed (not handwritten), with information matching Seller Central
- **Supplier credentials**: The supplier must be a manufacturer or authorized distributor
- **Letter of Authorization (LOA)**: For branded products
- **Safety certifications**: CE (EU), FCC (US), CSA (Canada)
- **Third-party lab test reports**: For regulated product categories

### Application Steps
1. Seller Central → Inventory → Add a Product
2. Search for the product you want to sell
3. Check the status indicator:
   - **"Sell this product"** — no approval needed
   - **"Apply to sell"** or **"Listing limitations apply"** — approval required
   - **"Unavailable"** — cannot be listed
4. Click "Request approval"
5. Submit the required documents
6. Wait 1-7 business days

## Prohibited Claims & Keywords

The following claims and keywords are strictly prohibited in listings:

### Health/Medical Claims (Most Strictly Enforced)
- Disease names: cancer, COVID-19, diabetes, HIV, etc.
- Treatment language: "cure," "treat," "heal," "medicine"
- Pesticidal/antimicrobial: "antibacterial," "antimicrobial," "virus," "disinfect"
- Weight loss/health: "detox," "keto," "weight loss" (without FDA approval)

### Prohibited in Listing Copy
- **Superlatives**: "best," "number one," "top rated," "award-winning"
- **Promotional/pricing language**: "free shipping," "limited-time offer," "clearance"
- **External references**: URLs, phone numbers, email addresses, QR codes
- **Intellectual property violations**: Unauthorized brand names, competitor references
- **R/™ symbols**: Using them in customer-facing content will cause the ASIN to be suppressed

## Marketplace Differences

| Product | United States | United Kingdom | Canada |
|------|------|------|--------|
| Baby walkers | ✅ Allowed | ✅ Allowed | ❌ Banned since 2021 |
| Infant sleep positioners | ❌ Banned by CPSC (SIDS risk) | ✅ Allowed | ❌ Banned |
| Antibacterial products | ❌ May be classified as pesticides | ✅ Allowed | ⚠️ Must be checked |
| Electronics | FCC certification | CE certification | CSA certification |

---

## Consequences of Violations

| Number of Violations | Penalty |
|---------|------|
| First violation | Listing removal + warning |
| Second violation (within 30-60 days) | Account deactivation |
| Severe violation (counterfeits, weapons, etc.) | Permanent account closure + funds frozen |
| Account deactivation appeal | Submit a Plan of Action (POA) within 7-10 days |

## Pre-Listing Checklist

1. ✅ Check whether the category is restricted on the target marketplace
2. ✅ Verify brand-level restrictions (major brands such as Nike, LEGO, Disney, and Samsung often have additional barriers)
3. ✅ Confirm country-specific restrictions (especially important when selling cross-border)
4. ✅ Prepare compliance documents: invoices, certifications, LOA
5. ✅ Review prohibited claims — no disease names, no superlatives, no unsubstantiated "natural" claims
6. ✅ Check product recall status (US CPSC, UK OPSS)
7. ✅ Ensure listing images are compliant — no badges, no QR codes, no watermarks
