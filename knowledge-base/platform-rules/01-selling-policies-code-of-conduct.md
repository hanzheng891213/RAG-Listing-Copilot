# Amazon Selling Policies & Code of Conduct

## Overview

The Amazon Seller Code of Conduct is a core companion document to the Business Solutions Agreement that all sellers sign when registering. It requires sellers to act fairly and honestly on Amazon to ensure a safe buying and selling experience. Violating the Code of Conduct may result in account deactivation.

Official help page reference: G1801

---

## Core Rules

### 1. Provide Accurate Information

You must provide accurate information to Amazon and our customers, and ensure that information is kept up to date:
- Business name must be accurate
- Product listing information must be truthful and accurate
- Shipping settings and inventory data must be kept current
- Do not provide false or misleading information

### 2. Act Fairly

The following unfair practices are prohibited:
- **Creating duplicate listings**: Do not create multiple detail pages for the same product
- **Manipulating sales rank**: Do not accept fake orders, or orders you paid for
- **Artificially inflating web traffic**: Do not use bots, paid clicks, or other methods to artificially increase traffic
- **Manipulating search results or keywords**: Do not manipulate search ranking through fake traffic or other means
- **Attempting to harm other sellers**: Do not attempt to harm other sellers, their products, or their ratings
- **Raising prices after order confirmation**: Do not raise the price of an item after an order confirmation is received
- **Malicious competition**: Do not file false infringement notices to remove a competitor's listing

### 3. Do Not Manipulate Ratings, Feedback, or Reviews

- Do not pay for reviews or offer any form of incentive in exchange for reviews
- Do not ask customers to write only positive reviews
- Do not ask customers to delete or modify negative reviews
- Do not write reviews for your own products or those of competitors
- Do not ask reviewers to include specific content in their reviews
- Do not exchange reviews for free products or other compensation

### 4. Communications

- All communication with customers must go through the Buyer-Seller Messaging system
- Do not send unsolicited marketing messages
- Do not include promotional links or offer information in communications
- Customer personal information (address, phone number) may be used only for order fulfillment purposes
- Customer personal information must be deleted after the order is fulfilled
- Do not use customer information for any other purpose

### 5. Do Not Circumvent the Sales Process

- Do not direct Amazon customers to external websites
- Do not provide links in listings or communications that lead to off-Amazon transactions
- Do not prompt customers to leave Amazon to make a purchase
- Do not use URLs, QR codes, or any other means to direct off-Amazon transactions

### 6. Multiple Selling Accounts

- You may have only one Seller Central account per region
- Exceptions (which require a legitimate business need):
  - Owning multiple independent brands
  - Selling for different business entities
  - Participating in Amazon programs that require separate accounts
- All accounts must remain in good standing

### 7. No Price Fixing

- Do not coordinate pricing with other sellers
- Do not engage in price fixing or other conduct that violates antitrust laws

### 8. Filing Infringement Notices

- Sellers with accounts in good standing may submit intellectual property infringement notices
- However, you may not submit infringement notices as an agent when doing so could benefit your own selling account (for example, to remove a competitor's listing)

---

## Consequences of Violations

| Violation Level | Penalty |
|---------|------|
| First violation | Listing removed + warning |
| Second violation (within 30-60 days) | Account deactivation |
| Severe violation | Permanent account closure + funds frozen |

## 2025-2026 Update Highlights

- **Appeal process update**: The fair conduct appeal page has changed to a questionnaire format instead of the traditional Plan of Action (POA) narrative
- **Proactive compliance**: Emphasis on regularly checking the Account Health Dashboard and auditing listings weekly
- **Top three violation causes**: Review manipulation, intellectual property infringement, and inaccurate information
- **Stronger enforcement**: Amazon is taking more aggressive action against Code of Conduct violations

---

## Best Practices

1. Check the Account Health Dashboard weekly
2. Ensure all product information is accurate and up to date
3. Audit listings regularly to ensure compliance
4. Follow policy update announcements in Seller Central
5. Establish standard operating procedures (SOPs) to prevent unintentional violations
