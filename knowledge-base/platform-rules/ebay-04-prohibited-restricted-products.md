# eBay Prohibited and Restricted Products

## Overview

For legal compliance and platform safety, eBay groups items into **prohibited**, **restricted**, and **potentially intellectual-property-infringing** categories, and the lists are not exhaustive. This document (based on the eBay US site; cross-border sales must also satisfy the buyer's country laws) covers prohibited/restricted categories, counterfeit goods and VeRO intellectual property rules, dangerous goods requirements, and the pre-listing compliance check. Violations bring listing removal and forfeited fees; repeated violations restrict selling privileges or permanently suspend the account, and serious cases may carry legal liability.

---

## Prohibited Items

The following items **may not be listed** (examples, not a complete list):

| Category | Description |
|------|------|
| **Drugs and paraphernalia** | Illegal drugs, prescription drugs, instructional content for manufacturing drugs |
| **Weapons and ammunition** | Firearms, ammunition, knives, imitation/toy guns, tear gas spray, stun guns |
| **Human organs and remains** | Human body parts and remains |
| **Recalled items** | Products recalled or posing a safety hazard |
| **Stolen goods** | Stolen items and items with obliterated serial numbers |
| **Infringing/violating electronics** | Cable descramblers, radar scanners, traffic signal control devices, wiretapping/eavesdropping equipment |
| **Offensive material** | Racially or ethnically discriminatory material, Nazi memorabilia |
| **Embargoed country goods** | Goods from sanctioned or embargoed countries (such as Cuba) |
| **Content encouraging illegal acts** | Books/ebooks describing illegal methods |

## Restricted Items

The following items **may be listed only when specific conditions are met**:

| Category | Example conditions |
|------|------------|
| **Alcohol and wine** | Requires the appropriate license and compliance with destination laws |
| **Tobacco and e-cigarettes** | Strictly restricted; prohibited or requiring credentials on most sites |
| **Adult products** | Must be in the dedicated adult category and must not appear in general search |
| **Prescription drugs and medical devices** | Contact lenses, pacemakers, surgical instruments, and similar |
| **Pesticides** | Must comply with local registration regulations |
| **Plants and seeds** | Subject to agricultural quarantine and import regulations |
| **Currency and precious metals** | Paper money, coins, gift cards, credit cards |
| **Tickets and securities** | Event tickets, stocks and securities, virtual currency |
| **Real estate** | Requires a dedicated category and format |
| **War/military items** | Military items and government- and transport-related items |
| **Surveillance equipment** | Electronic surveillance and eavesdropping devices |

> Tip: Conditions for restricted items change with the site and local laws; before listing, review the full policy page for the relevant eBay site.

## Counterfeit & Intellectual Property

| Rule | Description |
|------|------|
| **Counterfeits prohibited** | No selling counterfeits, replicas, fakes, or infringing goods |
| **VeRO program** | Rights owners can report infringing listings through the Verified Rights Owner program |
| **Report consequences** | Reported listings are removed; the seller receives the rights owner's contact information |
| **Genuine item ≠ free trademark use** | Even genuine items can draw a complaint if the listing misuses a trademark or image |
| **Relisting prohibited** | Removed items must not be relisted before the issue is resolved |
| **Appeal path** | Contact the rights owner with authorization or proof of authenticity (invoices, licenses, brand agreements), or await their reply and then contact eBay |
| **Compliant sourcing** | Keep purchase invoices and authorization documents; verify supplier credentials |
| **Use original assets** | Use your own photos and descriptions; avoid unauthorized trademarks and logos |

> VeRO applies only to genuine intellectual property infringement, not to business disputes such as minimum advertised price (MAP), selective distribution, or resale restrictions.

## Dangerous Goods and Regulated Items

| Category | Description |
|------|------|
| **Batteries** | Lithium batteries and products containing batteries have shipping and listing restrictions |
| **Fireworks and explosives** | Fireworks, firecrackers, and similar |
| **Refrigerants** | Regulated chemicals such as Freon |
| **Flammable/corrosive substances** | Hazardous chemicals, compressed gases |
| **Regulated materials** | Hazardous, restricted, or regulated materials that eBay may additionally restrict at its discretion |
| **Cross-border restrictions** | An item legal in the seller's country may be illegal in the buyer's; check import regulations too |

## Pre-Listing Compliance Checklist

- [ ] Confirmed the item is not on the prohibited items list
- [ ] If restricted, confirmed all conditions are met (license, category, destination laws)
- [ ] Confirmed the item is not counterfeit, a replica, or infringing
- [ ] Verified supplier credentials and retained purchase invoices
- [ ] Checked whether trademark, copyright, patent, or design rights are involved
- [ ] Checked whether the item is a dangerous good or regulated item
- [ ] Confirmed import and shipping compliance for cross-border sales
- [ ] Confirmed the payment service (such as credit card networks) rules allow the transaction
- [ ] Logged into the relevant eBay site to review the latest full policy page

---

## Consequences of Violation

| Violation type | Penalty |
|---------|------|
| Minor (incomplete information) | Listing hidden; restored after correction and review |
| Moderate (listing a restricted item or violating content) | Listing removal + warning + forfeited fees |
| Intellectual property infringement (VeRO) | Listing removal, loss of buyer/seller protection, lower search ranking, feedback removal |
| Repeated infringement | Temporary selling restriction (7 days/30 days), then permanent suspension |
| Serious violation | Account suspension, permanent removal of selling privileges, funds held, legal liability |

## Listing Compliance Checklist

### Prohibited items
- [ ] Not in a prohibited category such as drugs, weapons, stolen goods, or embargoed goods
- [ ] Not recalled
- [ ] No obliterated serial number

### Restricted items
- [ ] Confirmed the item is in a restricted category and its conditions are met
- [ ] Placed in the correct dedicated category
- [ ] Checked the law in the buyer's country

### Intellectual property
- [ ] Not a counterfeit or replica
- [ ] No misuse of another party's trademark or images
- [ ] Proof of purchase for genuine goods retained
- [ ] Removed items not relisted without authorization

### Dangerous goods
- [ ] Batteries/chemicals and similar confirmed as listable
- [ ] Shipping method confirmed compliant
- [ ] Cross-border import restrictions checked

### Pre-listing process
- [ ] Reviewed the latest policy on the relevant eBay site
- [ ] Confirmed payment service rules allow the sale
- [ ] Violation response and appeal process established
