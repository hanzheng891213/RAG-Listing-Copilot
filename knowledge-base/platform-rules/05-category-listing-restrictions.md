# Category Listing Restrictions

## Overview

Amazon applies gating to many product categories, meaning sellers must first pass a review before they can publish listings in restricted categories. Approval requirements vary by category, and high-value/high-counterfeit-risk categories have stricter requirements.

---

## General Approval Requirements (Shared by All Restricted Categories)

| Requirement | Description |
|------|------|
| **Professional seller account** | $39.99/month — individual seller accounts cannot sell in restricted categories |
| **Good performance metrics** | Order Defect Rate < 1%, pre-fulfillment cancel rate < 2.5%, late shipment rate < 4% |
| **Company website** | A verifiable business website (Squarespace/Wix is acceptable) |
| **Valid invoices** | Issued within the last 90-180 days, printed, with information matching the account |
| **Proof of product authenticity** | The supplier must be a manufacturer or licensed distributor |
| **Compliant product images** | Clearly showing the brand, labels, and packaging |

## Key Restricted Categories in Detail

### Jewelry — Highest Barrier

**Characteristics**: One of the hardest categories to get approved for on Amazon

**Fine jewelry (gold, silver, gemstones) requirements**:
- At least **1 year** of selling on Amazon
- Annual revenue of **$50,000 or more**
- At least one **physical store address**
- **Application fee** of $500-$1,000+
- Products may need to be sent to Amazon for **gemological testing** to verify authenticity

**Required documents**:
- Invoices issued by an authorized distributor
- Letter of Authorization (for brand-restricted jewelry lines)
- Proof of physical retail presence

---

### Beauty & Personal Care

**Characteristics**: A high-counterfeit-risk category

**Required documents**:
- Wholesale invoices from legitimate suppliers (issued within 90-180 days, 10+ units per product line)
- Product images showing the brand, labels, and UPC codes
- Safety/compliance documents:
  - FDA registration (for topical products containing active ingredients)
  - Certificate of Analysis
- Proof that products are authentic, safe, and compliantly labeled

**Special note**: Cosmetics, skincare/haircare products, and topical products generally require additional FDA-related documents

---

### Baby Products — Subcategories Vary by Marketplace

**Subcategories requiring approval**:
- Baby Feeding
- Baby Activity Gear
- Baby Strollers and Carriers

**Required documents**:
- Invoices from legitimate suppliers
- Safety testing documents
- **CPC (Children's Product Certificate)** — the key document for baby products
- ASTM F963 and CPSIA compliance proof

**2025 update**: Effective September 3, 2025, all children's toys and baby products must undergo retesting and verification every year

---

### Automotive & Powersports

**Required documents**:
- Professional seller account
- Good seller metrics
- A **company website** demonstrating a legitimate business
- **Compliant product images** shown on other websites
- Refurbished products must provide **warranty information**
- Specific fitment data requirements

**Note**: Automotive **accessories** are sometimes on the "commonly approved" list, but core automotive categories are restricted

---

### Grocery & Gourmet Food

**Required documents**:
- Invoices from approved suppliers
- FDA compliance documents
- Product label compliance
- Proof of shelf-life management

---

### Health & Personal Care

**Required documents**:
- Safety and labeling documents
- FDA registration (where applicable)
- GMP compliance proof
- COA required for dietary supplements

---

### Dietary Supplements & Over-the-Counter Drugs

**Required documents**:
- FDA facility registration
- Certificate of Analysis (COA)
- GMP compliance proof
- Ingredient list and label review

---

## Brand-Level Approval Barriers

Even when a category itself is unrestricted, specific brands may have additional barriers:

**Common restricted major brands**:
- Nike, Adidas, Under Armour
- Apple, Samsung, Sony
- LEGO, Disney, Hasbro
- Luxury brands: Louis Vuitton, Gucci, Chanel, etc.

**Brand authorization requirements**:
- A direct Letter of Authorization (LOA) from the manufacturer
- Invoices from an authorized distributor
- Explicit approval from the brand owner

## Application Process

### Step 1: Check the Category Status
Seller Central → Inventory → Add a Product → Search for the product → Check the status:
- **"Sell this product"** — no approval needed ✅
- **"Apply to sell"** — approval required ⚠️
- **"Unavailable"** — cannot be listed ❌

### Step 2: Prepare Documents
Gather all necessary invoices, certifications, and brand authorizations

### Step 3: Submit the Application
Click "Request approval" and upload the documents

### Step 4: Wait for Review
Typically 1-7 business days; may be approved instantly when the documents are complete

### Step 5: Handle a Rejection
- Read the rejection reason carefully
- Fix the issues and resubmit
- Do not repeatedly submit the same materials

## Maintaining Approval

- Approval can be revoked (if performance declines or policies are violated)
- Keep all compliance documents valid and up to date
- Check seller performance metrics regularly
- Follow category policy updates
- Resubmit documents when changing suppliers

---

## Common Rejection Reasons

| Rejection Reason | Solution |
|---------|---------|
| Expired invoice | Provide a new invoice issued within 90 days |
| Invoice information mismatch | Make sure the information on the invoice matches Seller Central |
| Unauthorized supplier | Obtain invoices from the manufacturer or an authorized distributor |
| Non-compliant product images | Provide compliant product images on a pure white background |
| Performance metrics below standard | Improve ODR, cancel rate, and late shipment rate |
| Missing safety documents | Supply CPC, FDA, CE, and other certifications |
